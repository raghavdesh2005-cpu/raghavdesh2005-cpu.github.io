Quantum fields and classical gravity

Hi, I’m Raghav. I’m a fourth-year physics student in the BS–MS programme at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal.

I work with Arnab Rudra on the classical limit of scattering amplitudes. I’m interested in what quantum field theory can tell us about the motion of two bodies interacting through gravity.

Research

When two massive bodies fly past each other, how much momentum does each gain or lose?

In classical mechanics, we can answer this by integrating the force along each trajectory. I study how to extract the same change in momentum, called the impulse, from a quantum scattering amplitude.

Technical details of my current project

I use the Kosower–Maybee–O’Connell (KMOC) framework to study the impulse in a weak-field expansion. At second post-Minkowskian order, the observable receives contributions from a one-loop amplitude and a term quadratic in tree amplitudes. I’m interested in how their apparent divergences cancel as ℏ tends to zero, leaving a finite classical impulse.
